## What we understand

Arkema is a specialty materials group of 20,700 employees in some 55 countries, with 2025 sales of
around €9.1 billion. Q2 2026 sales were €2,427.8m, up 1.3%, on organic growth of 3.2%: a 5.1%
price effect against volumes down 1.8%, currency negative 1.2%, scope negative 0.7%. EBITDA rose
7.4% to €390.9m and the margin gained 90 basis points to 16.1%. REBIT rose 10.9% to €219.5m, a
9.0% margin. Adjusted net income was €129.0m, €1.70 per share. Management describes a quarter
"impacted by the crisis in the Middle East, rising raw material prices and widespread supply chain
disruption", answered with "swift pricing adjustments". Europe stayed challenged, particularly
France and Germany, North America improved slightly, Asia held good momentum, and batteries,
electronics and 3D printing grew around 15%. Coating Solutions recovered to an 18.5% EBITDA margin
from 12.7% and Adhesive Solutions reached 15.1%, while Advanced Materials fell to 18.7% from 20.9%.

The pressure sits below the earnings line. Recurring cash flow fell 29.3% to €78.3m and free cash
flow 25.6% to €67.7m. Working capital rose to €1,537.8m from €1,134.3m at 31 December 2025, though
the ratio improved to 15.8% of annualized sales from 17.0% a year earlier. Net debt and hybrid
bonds of €3,604.9m stand at 2.9x last-twelve-months EBITDA, against 2.5x at 31 December 2025.
Capex was held to €117m from €152m, inside a €600m full-year limit.

## Topics for deeper conversation

**1. Two portfolio moves inside one quarter.** Arkema is ramping the recent acquisition of Dow
adhesives while having completed the divestment of small plastic additives businesses on 1 July,
already visible as the negative 0.7% group scope effect and a negative 4.2% sales impact in Coating
Solutions. Offering: **M&A Enablement** (IT Due Diligence, Integration Planning, Value Creation).
Proof is **Strong**. Aberdeen stood up a digital management office guiding post-merger IT
integration planning in a $4B transaction for a $5B+ provider, executed post-merger integration
for a private equity owner acquiring a diagnostic imaging equipment manufacturer, and ran buy-side
IT due diligence for a private equity acquirer.

**2. Five major projects ramping against a fixed capital ceiling.** In simultaneous ramp-up: Dow
adhesives, PA11 and Rilsan® Clear in Singapore, the PVDF expansion at Calvert City, new acrylics
distillation in France, and HF supply at Nutrien. They added around €25m of EBITDA in H1 and are
expected to deliver around €50m for the full year against 2025, inside the €600m capex limit.
Offering: **Large Program Assurance, Delivery, & Change**. Proof is **Strong**, and sits in
enterprise technology programs rather than industrial capital projects: implementation assurance
and project quality reviews across all initiatives of a large-scale transformation for a $5B+
provider, and ERP transformation for a global retailer serving customers in 23 different countries.

**3. Cost and cash discipline, including a two-site reorganization.** Arkema states an objective of
offsetting fixed cost inflation over the full year while "streamlining the organization". Operating
income of €141.4m included €46m of exceptional expenses, mainly the reorganization of the Jarrie
and Pierre-Bénite sites, legal proceedings and the divestment. Cash allocation stays a focus
through strict management of working capital and capex. Offerings: **IT Financial Mgmt. &
Optimization** (Cost Transparency, FP&A, Cost Transformation), **Financial Transparency** and
**Organizational & Operational Efficiency**. Proof is **Thin** and stated as such: these rest on
indirect evidence and imply no delivery history. The nearest quantified point is application
rationalization delivering $50m+ of savings over five years for a $500M–$999M provider.
