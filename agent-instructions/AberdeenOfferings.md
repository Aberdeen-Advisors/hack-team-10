## 5. Healthcare service lines — the six conversation topics

Grouped under three themes. This is the healthcare go-to-market structure.

### Theme A — How IT org is structured, led & governed

**1. Office of the CIO design & standup**
Building the structure, cadence, and visibility that positions the CDIO function as a
strategic business partner, not a service desk. The Aberdeen OCIO Model covers core
"Business of IT" services — IT Strategy, Vendor Mgmt., and related — to keep IT efficient
and transparent.

**2. OpModel & Org Design**
Aligning people, roles, partners, and accountability to actual digital value delivery.
Covers IT–operations alignment, IT org structure across diverse delivery models, and
leadership/layer design for span of control.

### Theme B — How patient & user-facing solutions are managed and modernized

**3. Epic Ecosystem**
Optimizing and extending the core clinical platform to close the gap between what Epic can
do and how clinicians actually work. Full detail in §6.

**4. Technology Portfolio Management**
Bringing discipline to how technology investments are selected, prioritized, and measured.
Includes **Application Rationalization** (five-step: Discovery → Analyze and Map
Applications → categorize retain/replace/retire → … → Develop a Rationalization Strategy and
Roadmap), **IT Demand Intake** (workflows and governance so investments are scoped and
evaluated against existing solutions), and **Project & Portfolio Management**.

### Theme C — How data and enabling platforms are secured and matured

**5. AI & Data**
Turning data into a governed, actionable asset supporting clinical decisions, operational
efficiency, and innovation at scale. Aberdeen "turns AI ambition into adopted, governed
capability." Includes **Building Trusted AI at Enterprise Scale** — the governance,
delivery, and adoption disciplines that move AI from isolated pilots to enterprise
capability — and **CARES: Governance In AI**, positioned on the premise that *"as AI moves
from assisting care providers to influencing care decisions, governance becomes a clinical
requirement — not simply a technology feature."*

**6. InfraOps**
Modernizing the underlying technology environment to be reliable, secure, and capable of
supporting the pace of digital transformation. Covers resilience, technical-debt reduction,
ITSM optimization, integration, and IT security strengthening the core.

## 11. How to use this file

- **Credential selection:** match the target's sector and pressure to §7–§8, lead with a
  quantified engagement, cite client type and revenue band rather than the name unless
  approval exists.
- **Offering recommendation:** map the detected need to §2, then check §10 for whether proof
  exists. If the row says Thin, sell the capability but do not imply a track record.
- **Deck construction:** follow `SKILL.md` §4.5 for the client-overview layout patterns, and
  use the Signal Cyan highlight mechanic to tailor §1 and §2 to the specific buyer.
- **Anonymisation:** default to *"Client is a [size] [sector] [type]…"* with the scale figure,
  per the qual page convention in `SKILL.md` §4.1.
